Unigox helps you receive and send business payments, build financial services into your product, and earn from your audience or business network. You can use one service for a specific task or combine several as your business grows.

Choose the section that matches what you need to do:

- Manage your company’s money.
- Add financial services to your product.
- Build a trading or money app.
- Pay suppliers in China.
- Earn from your audience and business network.

## Manage your company’s money

### Receive international payments

[Business accounts in Europe](https://www.unigox.com/european-business-accounts) give your company EUR, GBP and USD accounts in its legal name. Use them to collect customer payments and manage day-to-day business expenses through the Business Portal.

- Receive payments from international customers.
- Pay suppliers and partners in EUR or GBP.
- Track incoming and outgoing payments and download statements for your finance team.

Companies registered outside Europe can apply too. Eligibility depends on the country and business activity.

### Convert between fiat and stablecoins

The [OTC desk](https://www.unigox.com/otc-desk) handles larger conversions with your team. Use it when your customers pay in stablecoins but your costs are in EUR or GBP, or when you need to buy stablecoins to pay partners.

- Agree the amount, exchange rate, fees and receiving account or wallet before sending funds.
- Have Unigox carry out the conversion and send the proceeds to the agreed destination.
- Find confirmed deals in your Business Portal for your financial records.

OTC desks can also arrange settlement for their clients. This requires a separate arrangement, verification of the paying business and review of the payment before it is sent.

## Add financial services to your product

If you already run an app or platform, choose the service that fits how your customers need to move money: accounts in their own names, local deposits and withdrawals, or stablecoin payments.

### Offer accounts to your customers

You can also use Unigox’s [business account services](https://www.unigox.com/european-business-accounts) to issue accounts to your own customers. This lets them receive bank transfers through your product and gives you a way to earn from account services.

- Issue accounts in a business client’s legal name or an individual customer’s name.
- Manage account operations through the Business Portal or automate them through the API.
- Arrange issuance separately, with verification for your business and each account holder.

### Let users deposit and withdraw local currency

[Payments API](https://www.unigox.com/payments-api) connects your app to bank transfers and mobile money. Customers use familiar local payment methods, while your business sends and receives stablecoins. You build the customer experience and can add your own fee.

- **Deposits:** a customer pays in local currency to fund their balance; your business receives stablecoins.
- **Withdrawals:** your business funds the payment in stablecoins; the customer receives local currency in their bank account or mobile wallet.

**Keep your existing non-custodial infrastructure.** Wallets, neobanks and DeFi apps can add these payment flows without migrating users’ wallets or replacing their wallet provider.

### Accept stablecoin payments

[Crypto processing](https://www.unigox.com/crypto-processing) lets customers pay your business from a wallet or exchange. Use it for purchases, invoices or balance top-ups, with each payment linked to the right invoice or customer so your team does not have to match transfers by hand.

- **Connect your way:** integrate the API into your website or app, or use ready-made payment tools from Unigox.
- **Manage the proceeds:** keep stablecoins, send them to your wallet, or convert and withdraw to your bank account in a supported currency.

## Build a trading or money app

### Add asset trading to an existing app

[Trade API](https://www.unigox.com/trade-api) brings tokenized stocks, ETFs and meme coins into wallets, neobanks and trading apps. Your team builds the trading experience; Unigox handles blockchain execution.

- Users buy on supported networks with stablecoins they already hold, without separately bridging funds, buying a gas token or creating another wallet.
- Purchased assets arrive at the user’s existing address.
- Asset discovery, trending data and order updates help your app show what is available and follow each transaction.

You earn revenue from trades. Unigox-funded trading competitions can help engage users where local rules allow. Request access through the product page.

### Launch a complete app under your brand

[NeoBank-as-a-Service](https://www.unigox.com/neobank-as-a-service) is for teams that want a complete branded money app. It brings the app, payment connections and verification systems together, reducing the work of assembling them from separate providers.

- **App features:** local payments, a non-custodial crypto wallet and swaps.
- **Customer checks:** identity verification and transaction monitoring.
- **Launch and operations:** mobile app development, App Store submission support, payment infrastructure and operational support from Unigox.

You bring your brand, audience and business model; we agree the markets, services and launch scope together. A technology licence is also available if you already have your own regulatory licence and compliance team.

## Pay suppliers in China

For importers and companies buying from China, [Pay suppliers in China](https://www.unigox.com/pay-china) helps you find a payment route for a supplier’s invoice and check the estimated cost before paying.

- Choose your country and enter the amount the supplier should receive in CNY.
- Review the available payment methods and estimated cost.
- Fund with local payments or stablecoins, depending on your country and account.

For USD invoices, select the destination shown in your supplier’s bank details: mainland China, Hong Kong or Singapore. Check that route before arranging payment.

## Earn from your audience and business network

### Offer crypto trading on your website

If you run a crypto publication, community or website, the [crypto widget](https://www.unigox.com/crypto-widget) lets visitors buy and sell crypto alongside your content. Embed ready-made trading screens; Unigox provides the trading and customer verification flows.

- Earn **20% of Unigox’s trading fee** on completed buys and sells through your widget.
- Add your own markup on sell trades and adjust it in your partner dashboard.
- Track orders, statuses, completed volume and markup earned in the dashboard.

Withdraw earnings in crypto to your wallet, or convert to local currency using a payment method available in your country.

### Introduce businesses you already know

[Business referrals](https://www.unigox.com/sourcers) turns your existing business network into a source of recurring revenue. Offer contacts the service they need: business accounts, local payments, crypto processing or OTC conversions. The product range gives you several ways to help each client.

- **Introduce a client:** share your personal application link. Unigox handles business review and service setup.
- **Follow their progress:** see verification, services and activity in your Business Portal.
- **Earn from their activity:** receive **up to 20% of the revenue Unigox earns** from referred clients while they use the services covered by your partnership.

Your revenue share and payment terms are agreed before you start bringing clients.
